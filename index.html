    Terms of Agreement

    5. Payment Terms. The Initial Fee is due upon signing this Agreement, prior to commencement of work. 50% of the Total Development Budget is due upon signing; the remaining 50% is due upon delivery and handover of the completed website. The Monthly Maintenance Fee (MRR) is billed monthly in advance, starting from the website's go-live date. If payment is overdue by more than 7 days, the Agency reserves the right to suspend work and/or temporarily disable the live website until payment is received.

    6. Project Timeline. The timeline begins once the Initial Fee is received and all required content has been provided by the Client. Delays caused by late submission of content may extend the timeline accordingly.

    7. Revisions. The number of revision rounds included is determined by the selected package. Additional revisions or scope changes will be quoted and billed separately before work begins on them.

    8. Client Responsibilities. The Client agrees to provide timely feedback, content, and any access credentials necessary to complete the project within the agreed timeline.

    9. Ownership & IP. Upon full and final payment, all rights to the completed website design, custom code, and content created specifically for the Client become the property of the Client. Third-party assets remain subject to their own license terms.

    10. Confidentiality. Both Parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary business information shared during this engagement.

    11. Warranty & Support. The Agency provides a 30-day bug-fix warranty following launch. Ongoing support beyond this period is covered under the Monthly Maintenance Fee, where opted into.

    12. Termination. Either Party may terminate this Agreement with 15 days' written notice. The Client remains liable for payment of all work completed up to the termination date.

    13. Limitation of Liability. The Agency's total liability shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client under this Agreement.

    14. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan, unless otherwise agreed in writing by both Parties.
